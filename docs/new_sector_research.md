# New Career-Path Categories — Research Reference
Digital Career Hub · researched to fill the 4 near-empty categories identified in the category audit (Garments/Textile, NGO/Development, Agriculture/Environment, Healthcare/Pharma).

**How to read this:** every role below is tagged CONFIRMED (a real, named, dated source backs the figure/claim) or ESTIMATE (inferred — pattern across postings, a low-sample survey, or a stale source). Treat ESTIMATE as "needs a second check before publishing," per the client's explicit instruction: *"I need to know the source, if that's authentic source or not... if it is coming from somewhere, we need to acknowledge them."* bdjobs.com itself could not be fetched directly in this research pass (blocked); most postings were sourced via its syndication on The Financial Express / The Business Standard, or direct company career pages — each cited individually.

## REJECTED SOURCES (second research pass, for fields still NULL after the first pass)

Per the brief to fill every remaining empty salary field, a second pass searched payscale.com, paylab.com and similar aggregator sites directly for the roles still missing a number. Five results looked promising enough to open and check in full, and all five were rejected as unusable — not quietly skipped, but checked and found unreliable, for the reasons below. None of these numbers were used anywhere in the final data; every field they might have filled was instead filled with a labeled "(estimated — interpolated ...)" figure derived from already-CONFIRMED comparable roles already in this document.

- **Payscale.com Bangladesh — "Quality Assurance (QA) / Quality Control (QC) Inspector":** self-reported sample too small to be meaningful, with a range that does not hold together internally (effectively BDT ~1 to ~944,000/year on a sample in the low teens). A range spanning that wide on that few respondents is not a usable salary estimate.
- **Payscale.com Bangladesh — "Procurement Officer":** same shape of problem — very small sample (~12 profiles), range running from near-zero to roughly BDT 944,000/year.
- **Payscale.com Bangladesh — "Compliance Officer":** sample of about 6 profiles, range BDT 236,000–1,000,000/year — again too thin and too wide to anchor a figure on.
- **Payscale.com Bangladesh — "Industrial Engineer":** no stated sample size at all, which on this site has consistently correlated with the least reliable pages; used only as a soft cross-check, never as the source of a number (noted inline in the Garments/Textile QC row).
- **globalcostdata.com — "Agronomist" (and its other Bangladesh occupation pages):** confirmed by reading the page directly to be templated content, not measured wage data. The page states outright that it is "estimated from World Bank GNI per capita" because "verified ILO data [is] not yet available," and it contains a literal template-substitution grammar error — "a Agronomist" — that only happens when occupation names are mail-merged into a fixed sentence. This site was rejected wholesale, for every role, not just agronomist.

**Why interpolation instead of these numbers:** the client's own stated standard for this data (recorded meeting, Oct 8 2026 — "I need to know the source, if that's authentic source or not... if it is coming from somewhere, we need to acknowledge them") rules out presenting a tiny, self-reported, or templated-GNI figure as if it were a real wage. Importing any of the five numbers above would have reintroduced exactly the sourcing problem this whole project has been correcting. Interpolating from already-confirmed, same-sector, same-seniority roles already documented above is not itself a "source" either — it is a reasoned estimate, and every such field is labeled "(estimated — ...)" rather than CONFIRMED, with a one-line note on what it was interpolated from. These rows still need a human to spot-check them before going live, same as the rest of this draft.

## Salary-field reasoning, kept in full (the DB column truncated these to fit)

While wiring the draft migration into a real run, `salary_entry`/`salary_senior` on the hosted database turned out to be capped at 50 characters (narrower than the `TEXT` type the migration files describe — the live table apparently diverged from the tracked schema at some point). The original SQL packed the full reasoning into the value itself, e.g. `'BDT 18,000–25,000 (estimated — interpolated from comparably-skilled RMG roles; no QC-specific figure found)'`, which is well over the limit. ## New-sector resources: sourcing notes

3 resources per new discipline (Resume Writing, Job Search, Skill Development — Interview Prep and Soft Skills were left out since the existing "All disciplines" resources already cover that generic advice, and these 3 are where sector content actually differs). Every URL below was fetched directly and its real content checked against the title/description before use, per the client's sourcing standard. Full SQL: `add_new_sector_resources.sql`.

**Garments, Textile & Supply Chain**
- Resume Writing — [Merchandiser resume guide, Cake.me](https://www.cake.me/resources/resume/merchandiser-resume): role-specific (Merchandiser is a core RMG title) but a general international career site, not Bangladesh-authored — closest sector fit found.
- Job Search — [Bdjobs.com Garments/Textile category](https://bdjobs.com/h/jobs?lang=en&icatId=2): confirmed via live browser navigation, current postings including from BGMEA-affiliated employers.
- Skill Development — [BUFT programs](https://buft.edu.bd/programs): BGMEA's own university — as authoritative a source as this sector has.

**NGO & Development Sector**
- Resume Writing — [Devex development-CV guide, hosted by CU Boulder's Mortenson Center](https://www.colorado.edu/center/mortenson/sites/default/files/attached-files/devex_preparing_effective_development_cvs_0.pdf): sector-specific (international development) but not Bangladesh-specific — no good BD-local NGO-CV guide was found.
- Job Search — [ReliefWeb Jobs](https://reliefweb.int/jobs): confirmed live with 824 current results, filterable by country. BRAC's own careers.brac.net was checked as a BD-specific alternative but its job-listing content couldn't be independently confirmed (metadata-only fetch), so it was not used.
- Skill Development — [PM4R course, Inter-American Development Bank via Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/learn/project-management-for-development): confirmed live, explicitly framed for NGO/donor-funded project management.

**Agriculture & Environment**
- Resume Writing — [Farmers Weekly CV guide](https://www.fwi.co.uk/business/farming-cvs-eight-tips-writing-job-winning-resume): sector-specific (agriculture) but UK-focused — no Bangladesh-specific agriculture CV guide from a reputable source was found.
- Job Search — [BPSC government job circulars](https://bpsc.gov.bd/): official portal, covers BCS Agriculture cadre and DAE/BADC-relevant postings. Note: bdjobs.com could not be checked for this one (blocked by this session's fetch policy).
- Skill Development — [Modern Issues in Food & Agriculture, University of Illinois via Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/learn/modern-issues-in-food-and-agriculture): confirmed live, 1,871 enrolled.

**Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals**
- Resume Writing — [Pharmacy CV guide, The Pharmaceutical Journal (Royal Pharmaceutical Society)](https://pharmaceutical-journal.com/article/ld/writing-an-effective-cv-for-pharmacy-roles-a-guide-for-graduates): sector-specific but UK-context — no Bangladesh-specific pharma CV resource was found.
- Job Search — [Square Pharmaceuticals eRecruitment Portal](http://career.squarepharma.com.bd/): Bangladesh's largest pharma company's own portal, confirmed live with current QA/Production and sales openings.
- Skill Development — [Introduction to the Pharmaceutical Industry, University of Glasgow via Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/learn/introduction-to-the-pharmaceutical-industry): confirmed live, covers regulatory affairs/compliance and pharmacovigilance.

**Honest gap:** 3 of the 4 Resume Writing picks (Garments, NGO, Agriculture, Healthcare — all 4, really) are sector-specific but not Bangladesh-specific, because no reputable Bangladesh-authored CV guide exists yet for any of these sectors. Worth a note to the client rather than presenting these as more local than they are.
