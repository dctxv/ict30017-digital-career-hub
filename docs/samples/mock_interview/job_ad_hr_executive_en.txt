Meghna Apparels Group
Executive - Human Resources

Vacancy: 02
Job Location: Ashulia, Savar (Head Office) with occasional factory visits
Employment Status: Full-time
Salary: Negotiable
Application Deadline: 20 September 2026

Job Responsibilities
- Handle end-to-end recruitment for staff and management-level positions: posting,
  screening, interview scheduling, reference checks and offer letters
- Maintain the HRIS and personal files for around 1,200 head office and factory staff
- Process monthly attendance, leave and overtime data for payroll
- Support the compliance team during buyer audits (BSCI, Sedex) with HR records
- Organise onboarding and induction for new joiners
- Assist in preparing HR policies and circulars in Bangla and English
- Handle employee grievances at the first level and escalate where needed

Educational Requirements
- BBA or MBA with a major in Human Resource Management from a reputed university
- PGD in HRM from BIM or a similar institution will be an added advantage

Experience Requirements
- 1 to 3 years in an HR or administration role. Experience in the RMG sector is preferred.

Additional Requirements
- Good working knowledge of the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 and Labour Rules 2015
- Strong MS Excel skills (attendance and payroll data)
- Ability to communicate with factory workers as well as with foreign buyers' auditors
- Willing to work six days a week

Compensation and Other Benefits
- Mobile bill, lunch facilities, provident fund, two festival bonuses
