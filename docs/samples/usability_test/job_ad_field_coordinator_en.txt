BrightPath International - Bangladesh
Field Coordinator, Education Programme

Number of Positions: 01
Duty Station: Bandarban, with regular travel to Ruma and Thanchi upazilas
Contract: Full-time, one year, renewable based on performance and funding
Salary: As per organisation's salary scale
Application Deadline: 25 November 2026

About the Programme
BrightPath runs a mother-tongue based early education programme in the
Chittagong Hill Tracts with community schools in Marma, Tripura, Chakma and
Bawm languages. The Field Coordinator supports 30 community schools and their
teachers.

Key Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate field activities with community school teachers and school
  management committees
- Build working relationships with headmen, karbaris, union parishad members and
  upazila education offices
- Monitor school attendance and learning data and check its quality
- Facilitate parent meetings and community events in local languages
- Write monthly progress reports and short case stories in English
- Make sure safeguarding and child protection policies are followed

Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Social Science, Education or a related subject;
  Master's will be an advantage
- At least 2 years of community work, preferably in the Chittagong Hill Tracts

Skills and Competencies
- Fluency in at least one CHT indigenous language (Marma, Tripura, Chakma or Bawm)
  together with Bangla
- Good written and spoken English
- Experience with mobile data collection (KoboToolbox or ODK)
- Able to ride a motorcycle and travel to remote areas
- Understanding of child protection and safeguarding

Benefits
- Festival bonuses, provident fund, health insurance
- Field allowance and motorcycle provided

How to Apply
