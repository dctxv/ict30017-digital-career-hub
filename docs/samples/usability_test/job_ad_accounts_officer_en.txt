Meridian Pharmaceuticals Ltd.
Accounts Officer (Finance and Accounts)

Vacancy: 01
Job Location: Tejgaon Industrial Area, Dhaka
Employment Status: Full-time
Salary: Negotiable

Job Context
Meridian Pharmaceuticals manufactures generic medicines for the local market
and exports to six countries. The Finance and Accounts department is moving its
books from Tally to SAP S/4HANA, and we need an Accounts Officer who can keep
day-to-day accounting accurate during the change.

Job Responsibilities
- Record vendor invoices and payments and keep the accounts payable ledger up to date
- Prepare monthly bank reconciliations for five bank accounts
- Prepare VAT and AIT (advance income tax) schedules and support the monthly returns
- Help prepare month-end and year-end schedules for the external auditors
- Support the SAP migration by checking opening balances and master data
- Follow internal controls and keep supporting documents in order

Educational Requirements
- BBA or MBA in Accounting or Finance
- Part-qualified CA or CMA will be preferred

Experience Requirements
- 2 to 4 years in accounts, preferably in a manufacturing company

Additional Requirements
- Good understanding of IFRS and Bangladesh VAT and income tax rules
- Experience with an ERP system; SAP experience will be an advantage
- Strong Excel skills
- Able to work under pressure at month end
- Willing to relocate to Dhaka

Compensation and Other Benefits
- Provident fund, gratuity, two festival bonuses
- Group life and health insurance
- Lunch facilities and transport support
