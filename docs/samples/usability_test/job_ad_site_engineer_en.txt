Delta Infrastructure Ltd.
Site Engineer (Civil)

Vacancy: 04
Job Location: Rajshahi, Bogura and Naogaon (project sites)
Employment Status: Full-time
Salary: BDT 35,000 - 50,000
Application Deadline: 10 November 2026

Job Context
Delta Infrastructure builds rural roads, bridges and drainage for government and
private clients across north Bengal. We are hiring site engineers to run day-to-day
work on three new road and culvert projects.

Job Responsibilities
- Supervise daily construction work on site as per drawings and specifications
- Prepare and check BOQ, measurement sheets and running bills
- Check quality of materials and workmanship, and keep test records
- Plan daily work for labour and subcontractors and report progress to the project manager
- Make sure safety rules are followed on site
- Read and prepare AutoCAD drawings when needed

Educational Requirements
- Diploma in Civil Engineering or B.Sc. in Civil Engineering

Experience Requirements
- At least 5 years for Diploma holders, 2 years for B.Sc. holders
- Road, bridge or culvert experience will be preferred

Additional Requirements
- Good knowledge of AutoCAD, BOQ and estimating
- Knowledge of the Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC) and site safety
- Able to use a total station or auto level
- Must be willing to stay at project sites outside the city
- Age 25 to 40 years

Compensation and Other Benefits
- Site allowance and accommodation at site
- Mobile bill, two festival bonuses
- Yearly salary review
