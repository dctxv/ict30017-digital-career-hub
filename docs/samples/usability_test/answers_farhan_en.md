## About me

I graduated in 2025 with a B.Sc. in Statistics from Jahangirnagar University,
CGPA 3.38. For my thesis I surveyed 220 households in Dhaka about electricity use
and ran regression in SPSS and R. After that I did a four-month internship at
Shapla Microfinance Foundation, where I made monthly Excel reports for branch
managers. I enjoy finding the story in the numbers, and I want to do that full
time as a data analyst.

## Working in a team

For my thesis survey we were three students. One teammate kept missing our field
days, so the two of us were doing all the visits. Instead of complaining to our
supervisor, I asked him what was going on. He had a part-time tuition job on
those days. We changed the plan so he did data entry in the evenings instead of
field visits. We finished the survey on time and everyone did a fair share.

## A mistake I made

At my internship, one monthly report showed a branch with almost double its
normal collections. I nearly sent it, but it looked wrong, so I checked. Two field
offices had entered the same week twice. I removed the duplicates and fixed the
report before it went out. Since then I always check totals against last month
before I send anything.
