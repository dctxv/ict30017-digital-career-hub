PayNest Ltd.
Customer Service Executive (Contact Centre)

Vacancy: 15
Job Location: Tejgaon, Dhaka
Employment Status: Full-time
Salary: BDT 22,000 - 28,000
Application Deadline: 05 December 2026

Job Context
PayNest is a mobile wallet used by over two million people in Bangladesh to send
money, pay bills and shop. Our contact centre answers customers by phone, live
chat and social media, seven days a week.

Job Responsibilities
- Answer customer calls and chats about payments, failed transactions and account issues
- Solve problems on the first contact where possible, and escalate the rest correctly
- Log every case in the CRM system with clear notes
- Stay calm and polite with upset customers
- Meet targets for response time and customer satisfaction

Educational Requirements
- Graduate in any discipline

Experience Requirements
- Freshers can apply. Experience in customer service, sales or teaching will be
  an advantage.

Additional Requirements
- Excellent spoken Bangla and good spoken and written English
- Comfortable using computers and typing at least 25 words per minute
- Willing to work rotating shifts, including weekends (transport provided for
  night shifts)
- Women candidates and career returners are encouraged to apply

Compensation and Other Benefits
- Two festival bonuses, shift allowance, lunch
- Performance bonus every quarter
- Four weeks of paid training before going live

Read Before Apply
Apply through the online application on our website, or email your CV as a PDF
to careers@paynest.example with the subject "CSE Contact Centre".
