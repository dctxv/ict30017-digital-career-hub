Northstar Retail Ltd.
Junior Data Analyst

Vacancy: 02
Job Location: Gulshan-1, Dhaka
Employment Status: Full-time
Salary: BDT 30,000 - 40,000 (negotiable)
Application Deadline: 15 November 2026

Job Context
Northstar Retail runs 28 grocery and household outlets across Dhaka and
Chattogram, plus a home delivery service. Our small data team turns sales and
stock data into reports that store and category managers use every week. We
are hiring two junior analysts to grow with the team.

Job Responsibilities
- Write SQL queries to pull sales, stock and delivery data from our database
- Clean and check data in Excel or Python before it is reported
- Build and update dashboards in Power BI for store and category managers
- Prepare a weekly sales summary and explain the main changes to managers
- Help with ad hoc questions, such as which products sell slowly in which outlets
- Keep clear notes so that another analyst can repeat your work

Educational Requirements
- B.Sc. or BBA in Statistics, Economics, Mathematics, Computer Science or a
  related subject

Experience Requirements
- 0 to 1 year. Fresh graduates with internship or project experience are
  encouraged to apply.

Additional Requirements
- Working knowledge of SQL (SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY) is required
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Strong Excel skills, including pivot tables and lookups
- Python (pandas) is a plus
- Able to explain numbers clearly to non-technical managers, in Bangla and English
- Careful with detail; you check your own work before it goes out

Compensation and Other Benefits
- Two festival bonuses, mobile bill, lunch facilities
- Weekly two holidays
- Paid training on Power BI in the first three months

Read Before Apply
Email your CV as a single PDF to careers@northstar-retail.example with the subject
line "Junior Data Analyst". Shortlisted candidates will take a short Excel and SQL
test before the interview.
