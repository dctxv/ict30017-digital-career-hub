# Interview answer notes

## About me

I studied Anthropology at the University of Chittagong. For the last two years I
have worked in the field in Rangamati: first on a livelihood survey, now on an
adolescent health project in 18 villages. I speak Chakma, Bangla and English,
and I want to coordinate a programme, not just deliver sessions.

## Working with communities

Before starting sessions in a new village, I always meet the headman and karbari
first and explain the plan. In one village parents did not want their daughters
to attend health sessions. I invited the mothers to the first session and ran it
with a female teacher they trusted. After that, attendance went from 6 girls
to 21.

## A data problem I solved

During the livelihood survey, some forms in KoboToolbox had impossible answers,
like a household of 3 with 7 school-age children. I checked the submissions
every evening, found it was one enumerator, and sat with him the next morning to
go through the questions again. The errors stopped.

## Languages (be honest)

- Chakma is my mother tongue, and I am fluent in Bangla and good in English.
- I do not speak Marma or Tripura, which are common in Bandarban. I can follow

## Why this job

Mother-tongue education matters to me. I started school in Bangla without
understanding most of what the teacher said. A programme that teaches children in
their own language first is the kind of work I want to do.

## Strength and weakness
