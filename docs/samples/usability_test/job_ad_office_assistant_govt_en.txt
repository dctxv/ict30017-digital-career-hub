Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh
Office of the District Education Officer, Noakhali
Recruitment Circular (sample)

Post: Office Assistant cum Computer Typist
Number of Posts: 03
Pay Scale: Grade 16 (BDT 9,300 - 22,490), National Pay Scale 2015
Application Deadline: 30 November 2026

Educational Qualification
- HSC or equivalent pass from a recognised board (Alim and Diploma accepted)

Other Requirements
- Typing speed: at least 20 words per minute in Bangla and 20 words per minute
  in English
- Knowledge of computer operation (MS Word, MS Excel, email)
- Age: 18 to 32 years as of the date of this circular

Duties
- Type letters, notices and reports in Bangla and English
- Maintain files, registers and records of the office
- Receive and dispatch letters
- Assist officers with data entry and other office work

Selection Process
- Written test, practical test (computer and typing) and viva voce

How to Apply
Fill in the prescribed online application form on the Teletalk recruitment
website. Upload a recent colour photograph and signature as instructed in the
form. Application fee must be paid through Teletalk prepaid SIM. No CV will be
accepted by post or email.
