### **Key Changes and Rationale:**

1.  **"SUMMARY" Section:**
    *   **Rationale:** Replaced "CAREER OBJECTIVE" with a "SUMMARY" or "PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY." Objectives are often seen as outdated. A summary provides a concise overview of your qualifications, skills, and career aspirations, immediately telling the recruiter what you bring to the table.
    *   **Content:** Highlights your degree, key skills, and internship experience, directly linking them to your career goal.

2.  **Contact Information Formatting:**
    *   **Rationale:** Made it more compact and professional, typically placed at the top.

3.  **Action Verbs in Experience:**
    *   **Rationale:** Used stronger action verbs (e.g., "Assisted," "Supported," "Organized," "Performed," "Aided") to describe responsibilities, making your contributions sound more impactful.
    *   **Quantifiable/Specific Details:** Added minor details like "ensuring accuracy and efficiency" and "maintaining up-to-date client information" to give more context to your tasks.

4.  **"SKILLS" Section:**
    *   **Rationale:** Separated "COMPUTER SKILLS" and "LANGUAGE PROFICIENCY" into a more comprehensive "SKILLS" section.
    *   **Added "Core Competencies":** This is crucial for entry-level candidates. It allows you to list soft skills and specific tasks you're proficient in that are relevant to banking/finance, even if not explicitly taught in a course. This helps with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

5.  **Removed "Signature" Line:**
    *   **Rationale:** In digital resumes, a physical signature is not required. The name at the bottom of the declaration is sufficient.

6.  **Collapsible "PERSONAL INFORMATION" Section:**
    *   **Rationale:** While this information is often included in Bangladeshi resumes, it's generally considered less critical for the initial screening process in many international contexts. Making it collapsible allows the recruiter to focus on your professional qualifications first, but still provides the information if needed.

7.  **Overall Formatting:**
    *   **Headings:** Used clear, bold headings for each section.
    *   **Bullet Points:** Consistently used bullet points for readability.
    *   **Dates:** Standardized date formats.
